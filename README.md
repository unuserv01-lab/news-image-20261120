One image that sums up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026: a newspaper front page, THE WORLD LEDGER, with 35 verified stories from seven regions. Lead: Russian ballistic missiles damage three Dnipro bridges in Kyiv (110+ missiles, ~400 drones, nine killed). Also leading: Cyclone Akvan makes landfall in Odisha with 300,000+ evacuated; Asia's AI sell-off (Nikkei −5.1%, Kospi −5.2%, Taiex −6.2%, SoftBank −16%); the UK–EU reset deal (£17bn a year, youth mobility for 100,000); and COP31's final day in Antalya, where the draft drops the fossil-fuel phase-out. A markets strip carries the day's falls and Anthropic's record $81.3bn IPO (+17%), a UTC timeline tracks how the day unfolded, and every figure is attributed to reporting from 20 November 2026 (Reuters, BBC, AP, AFP, FT, Kyiv Independent, WHO, JTWC, ECDC). The program is a deterministic one-line copy: it writes the image to /output/entry.png, byte for byte identical on every run, with no network.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
